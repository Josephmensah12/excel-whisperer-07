# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two groups, weighted about equally. Ghanaians living anywhere in the US sending a car, barrel or
boxes home to family, often first-time shippers deciding whether to trust a company they found on
Google or through a WhatsApp group; and repeat buyers/exporters shipping several vehicles or
commercial loads who care about price, speed and paperwork competence. Most arrive on a phone.

## Product Purpose

goldcoastgloballogistics.com is the lead-generation front door for Gold Coast Global Logistics,
a Houston-based shipper to Tema/Accra, Ghana. Customers anywhere in the US reach us by inland
transport to our Houston warehouse. Success = a visitor leaves contact details (quote, call,
vehicle estimate) that customer service turns into a booking. Search and AI-answer-engine
visibility is the main acquisition channel and must never be put at risk.

## Brand Personality

Clean, premium, dependable. Calm confidence of a working shipping business, not startup hype.
The voice is plain and personal ("we'll call you"), specific (real port names, real warehouse
address, real steps) and honest about what an estimate is.

## Anti-references

- Anything that looks AI-generated: centred hero + icon tile + three bullets; rows of identical
  icon/heading/text cards; stat-tile rows; gradient text; glassy cards.
- Generic "logistics template" sites (stock globe, container stack, navy-and-gold everywhere).
- Stock photos used as filler; decoration without information.

## Design Principles

1. Show the customer's own situation (their city, their car, their route) instead of describing the service.
2. One moment per page: each page has one thing that matters most, and the design points at it.
3. Real shipping detail is the texture: routes, port names, documents, addresses beat icons.
4. Brand colours used with restraint: navy ink, warm neutrals, gold reserved for the key moment.
5. Honest by design: estimates look like estimates; what's not included is said plainly.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Phone-first (375px) with no horizontal scroll, 16px+ body text, visible focus,
labelled form fields, respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. Many users are older or on older phones.
